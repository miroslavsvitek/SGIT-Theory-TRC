# Reproducibility protocol

The canonical graph has 64 nodes, 222 oriented edges, 216 oriented triangles, rank(B1)=63, rank(B2)=153, and six harmonic directions. Twelve fixed eigenvectors from each of gradient and curl sectors are selected; original matrices are at source commit `f0ffc52cf0003bdba2e90ca53712375beda6709d` in https://github.com/miroslavsvitek/SGIT-Hodge-Curl-Conscious-Visual-Detection .

The signed relation between channels is `(xi0*xj1-xj0*xi1)/(sqrt((xi0²+xi1²)*(xj0²+xj1²))+1e-12)`. The stabilizer is added outside the square root as in the original code. The 222 edge relations are projected into fixed selected 12 CURL and 12 GRAD eigenvectors. Window energies at 80–240, 240–400 and 400–560 ms are normalized within each window. A separate three-window tensor comprises 12³=1,728 entries with symmetric dimension 364, alternating dimension 220 and mixed-symmetry dimension 1,144. Preserved-pair shuffles keep two windows paired and reassign the third within each participant and condition.

This is a sensor-level mathematical descriptor. It is not a direct measurement of cortical circulation, functional causality, or a cortical current flow. The 30+35-person visual replication and 27-person archived trial-feature results are cited as historical reports; this companion branch does not contain all original participant-level arrays. The October 2026 frequency-triad and modulation controls were negative and exploratory, with 28-person interpolation in the transferred dataset. Their full original configuration and output archives accompany the locally delivered reproducibility ZIP.
